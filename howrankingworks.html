How ratings work

X

Everyone starts equal

Your skill level is determined exclusively by the results you earn on the court. There are no self-assigned ratings here.

X

How your rating moves

Winning against higher-rated players gives your score a bigger boost. Beating a lower-rated team will result in a smaller gain.

X

Reliability

When you first join, your rating will swing up and down quickly. As you log more matches, your rating settles and becomes more accurate.

X

It's doubles

The algorithm looks at the combined strength of both teams. Pulling off an upset against a tough team earns you a larger rating increase.

X

Keeping it fair

Both teams must confirm every match to make it count. We enforce one account per phone number, and beating the same opponents repeatedly yields fewer points.
